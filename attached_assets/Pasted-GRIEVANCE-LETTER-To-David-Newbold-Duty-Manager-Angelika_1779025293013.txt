GRIEVANCE LETTER

To: David Newbold (Duty Manager)
& Angelika Lenczewska (Operations Manager)
From: Ian Moon
Date: 16th May 2026
Subject: Formal Grievance Regarding Management Conduct and Unfounded Accusations

1. Introduction

I, Ian Moon, wish to formally raise a grievance regarding an incident that occurred on 16th May 2026 during my scheduled shift. On this date, I was approached by two members of the management team—one from my shift and one from the late shift—in a manner which I found unprofessional and inappropriate.

2. Details of the Incident

During this incident, my shift manager accused me of not performing any work for the previous two hours, which is factually incorrect. During that time, I had:

Changed batteries for Lollop reach trucks and PPT units in line with my duties
Thoroughly cleaned and maintained the battery bay

The accusation appears to have arisen because 4–5 call-outs were made for me to attend the pick desk, which I did not hear. The tannoy system does not function within the battery bay, an issue formally raised with management on multiple occasions without resolution.

When I attempted to explain this, my shift manager responded dismissively:

“I am not interested; you have done nothing for the last two hours.”

This response was unfair, dismissive, and unprofessional.

3. Legal and Procedural Concerns

I believe this conduct may constitute:

Unfounded accusations and unfair treatment, contrary to the Employment Rights Act 1996, which protects employees against detrimental treatment arising from workplace disputes.
Failure to provide a safe and supportive working environment, in line with the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, as communication systems are essential for staff safety and operational efficiency.
Potential breaches of the ACAS Code of Practice on Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures, which requires that allegations be handled fairly, investigations conducted properly, and employees given the opportunity to respond.
4. Requested Actions

In light of the above, I formally request the following:

A full investigation into the conduct of the management team involved in this incident
Correction of any records or documentation that incorrectly state I failed to perform my duties
Additional training or guidance for management to ensure workplace disputes and communication issues are handled professionally in the future
Remedial action regarding the tannoy system in the battery bay to prevent similar misunderstandings and ensure employee safety
5. Conclusion

I am willing to provide any further information or clarification to support this grievance and request that I am kept informed of the investigation process and outcomes in accordance with company policy and best practice.

Thank you for your attention and prompt action on this matter.

Yours sincerely,
Ian Moon
